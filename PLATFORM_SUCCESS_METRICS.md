# Sanatan Marga - Platform Success Metrics
*(Key Performance Indicators & Executive Dashboard Metrics)*

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) to monitor platform health, editorial effectiveness, community safety, and business viability as Sanatan Marga moves into Pilot Launch phase.

---

## 1. Editorial KPIs (Journalistic Excellence)
*Focus: Measuring the efficiency and quality of news production.*

| Metric | Description | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Articles Published** | Total volume of content published daily/weekly. | `/admin/analytics` |
| **Assignment Completion Rate** | % of assignments completed by deadline. | `/admin/operations` |
| **Average Publication Time** | Time elapsed from draft start to publishing. | `/admin/operations` |
| **Fact Check Completion Rate** | % of articles verified by the AI newsroom/editorial gate. | `/admin/analytics` |

---

## 2. Community KPIs (Safety & Engagement)
*Focus: Measuring health, trust, and safety within the Chautari civic circles.*

| Metric | Description | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Active Users (DAU)** | Unique users engaging with the platform daily. | Firebase Analytics |
| **Posts Created** | Total volume of community-authored content. | `/admin/analytics` |
| **Reports Submitted** | Volume of community-flagged items. | `Moderation OS` |
| **Appeal Rate** | % of bans appealed by community members. | `Moderation OS` |
| **Moderator Resolution Time** | Average time to process a report/appeal. | `Moderation OS` |

---

## 3. Business KPIs (Monetization & Sustainability)
*Focus: Tracking growth and viability for future Membership/Revenue OS.*

| Metric | Description | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Campaign Revenue** | Gross revenue generated from sponsorships. | Revenue OS (Planned) |
| **Advertiser Retention** | % of sponsors renewing their campaign contracts. | Revenue OS (Planned) |
| **Newsletter Subscribers** | Total active reach for outbound mailers. | Notification OS |
| **Membership Conversions** | % of users upgrading to paid tiers. | Membership OS (Planned) |
